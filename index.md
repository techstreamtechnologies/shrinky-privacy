_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

## Summary

Shrinky processes your images and PDFs entirely on your device. We do not run a server, we do not have user accounts, and we never upload your photos or documents.

## Files you choose

When you pick an image, Android gives the app access to that file only, through the system photo picker or file picker. The app makes a temporary copy in its private cache to work on. Your original file is never modified or deleted. Temporary copies and outputs are deleted when the app next starts. Files are written to your Pictures or Download folder only when you tap Save.

## Image metadata

Exported images do not include EXIF metadata such as GPS location, camera model or capture time. This information is not read for any other purpose and is not sent anywhere.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings and, if enabled, a list of files you saved (file name, type, size, date and folder). This list stays on your device and can be cleared or turned off in Settings.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, app interactions with ads, and diagnostics. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## What we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive your images, PDFs, file names, file paths, EXIF data, contacts, or account information. The app has no analytics of its own.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to collect information from them.
